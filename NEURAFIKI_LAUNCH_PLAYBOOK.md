## MONTH 2: PILOT PROGRAMS & REFINEMENT

### WEEK 5-6: PARENT CIRCLE COHORT 1 LAUNCH

**Structure**:
- **Duration**: 6 weeks
- **Frequency**: Weekly 60-minute sessions
- **Time**: Choose based on parent availability
- **Format**: Zoom (recorded for on-demand access)
- **Size**: 25-50 parents per cohort
- **Cost**: Free for pilot (or $15 pilot price)

**Session 1: "Finding Your Footing After Diagnosis"**
- Welcome & circle building
- Share your story (brief intro)
- Common emotions after diagnosis
- First steps forward
- Resource sharing
- Homework: Identity reflection exercise

**Session 2: "Understanding Your Child's Communication"**
- Communication beyond words
- Sensory needs & expression
- Recognizing progress (small victories)
- Home communication strategies
- Video demonstration
- Homework: Implement one strategy

**Session 3: "Building Routines That Work"**
- Why routines help neurodivergent kids
- Creating sensory-friendly schedules
- Visual supports
- Transitions & flexibility
- Common routine pitfalls
- Homework: Design one routine

**Session 4: "Managing Overwhelm & Self-Care"**
- Parental burnout reality
- Emotional regulation for parents
- Building support networks
- Setting boundaries
- Community resources
- Homework: Self-care plan

**Session 5: "School Conversations That Work"**
- Preparing for school
- Teacher partnerships
- Advocacy without antagonism
- IEP/Education plan navigation
- Questions to ask
- Homework: School prep checklist

**Session 6: "Building Your Support Network"**
- Finding your people
- Community vs. isolation
- Mentor relationships
- Faith & spirituality (optional)
- Celebrating progress
- Graduation & next steps

**Materials Provided**:
- Parent workbook (6-week guide)
- Session notes & recordings
- Resource templates
- Progress journals
- Community directory

**Facilitator Role**:
- Create safe space
- Share expertise selectively
- Elevate parent wisdom
- Document stories (with permission)
- Gather feedback for refinement
