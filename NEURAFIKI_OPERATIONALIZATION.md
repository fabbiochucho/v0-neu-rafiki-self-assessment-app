## PART 3: PROGRAM LAUNCH SEQUENCE

### PHASE 1: COMMUNITY MOVEMENT (Month 1-2)
**Goal**: Build audience, establish authority, create cultural shift

**Milestones**:
- Week 1: Publish founding story across all channels
- Week 2: Launch story series (Story 1)
- Week 3: Release first educational guide
- Week 4: Host first webinar/live session
- Week 5-8: Weekly story releases + community engagement

**Metrics**:
- Story reach (target: 10,000 unique readers)
- Email subscribers (target: 500)
- Social media growth (50% increase)
- Website traffic (target: 5,000 monthly visitors)

---

### PHASE 2: PARENT CIRCLE PILOT (Month 2-3)
**Goal**: Test community format, build testimonials, refine curriculum

**Execution**:
- Recruit 25-50 parent pilots
- Weekly 60-min facilitated sessions (cohort-based)
- Focus groups for feedback
- Document success stories

**Sessions Focus**:
1. "Finding Your Footing After Diagnosis"
2. "Understanding Your Child's Communication"
3. "Building Routines That Work"
4. "Managing Overwhelm and Self-Care"
5. "School Conversations That Work"
6. "Building Your Support Network"

**Deliverables**:
- Curriculum doc
- Facilitator guide
- Parent workbook
- Video recordings for on-demand access

---

### PHASE 3: HOME TOOLKIT BETA (Month 3)
**Goal**: Release first version, gather user feedback

**Deliverables**:
- 5 core toolkits (communication, sensory play, routines, emotional regulation, school prep)
- Video tutorials for each toolkit
- Customizable templates
- Progress journals

**Distribution**:
- Free download on website
- Email to subscriber list
- Share with school partners
- Community promotion

---

### PHASE 4: SCALE PARENT CIRCLE (Month 4-6)
**Goal**: Open to broader audience, establish as flagship program

**Structure**:
- Monthly cohorts (50-100 parents each)
- Multiple languages (if possible)
- Regional facilitators trained
- Hybrid (online + local meetups)

**Growth Strategy**:
- Word-of-mouth from pilot
- School partnerships
- Health system referrals
- Community organization partnerships

---

### PHASE 5: SCHOOL INITIATIVE LAUNCH (Month 5-6)
**Goal**: Partner with 5-10 schools for pilot

**Approach**:
- Identify schools (mix of inclusive + excluding schools)
- Train 30-50 teachers
- Implement pilot programs
- Document outcomes

**Components**:
- 8-week teacher training program
- School action planning
- Student outcomes tracking
- Teacher confidence metrics
